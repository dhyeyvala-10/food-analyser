About Food Analyser

🎯

Our Mission

To empower consumers with transparent, easy-to-understand information about food ingredients, helping them make healthier choices for themselves and their families.

🔬

How It Works

Our analysis engine categorizes ingredients, evaluates their health impact based on scientific research, and presents the information in a clear, hierarchical format.

📊

Safety Ratings

Each ingredient is rated on a scale of 1-10 based on safety, health benefits, and potential concerns. The overall score reflects the product's health profile.

💡

Color Coding

Green = Safe/Beneficial, Yellow = Moderate Concern, Red = Potential Health Concerns

Disclaimer: This tool provides general information about food ingredients. It should not replace professional medical advice or be used as the sole basis for dietary decisions. Always consult with healthcare professionals regarding specific health concerns.
